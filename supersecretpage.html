Hello! I am Aaron Cainglet.

I am a senior at Carson High School in the ESET academy. This year, I am graduating from high school to college. I am attending El Camino College to study Automotive Engineering/Design. Ever since I was a child, I had always loved cars and often drew them in my spare time. I draw cars that exist in real life, and even design some of my own cars. While I enjoy the pen to paper media, I also enjoy 3D modelling cars in the popular modelling software, Blender. Whenever I am not drawing or making 3D models, I am usually listening to music by Lovejoy, playing video games, driving, biking, a little bit of photography and even video creation.

"Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish."

-Steve Jobs, Apple Founder (2005)                                   

Why ESET?

ESET is an acronym, meaning "Environmental Science Engineering and Technology." I chose ESET because I want to apply a creative engineering mindset into products that are sustainable and Eco-friendly to the environment. My career goal is to create innovative products with the latest cutting edge technology that puts a positive impact on the lives of people around the world.

ESET has helped me develop a creative engineering mindset and opened my eyes to being more environmentally friendly. It made me understand how to think like an engineer, how to solve problems using critical thinking, and how to bring an idea from concept to life in a team-working environment.

How have I grown as a person?

As I entered High School and into 9th grade, I was a shy person. I preferred to not do presentations and keep to myself and mind my own business. Although this was the case, I made it a goal for myself to improve my communication skills. Throughout High School, I kept this goal in mind and learned how to communicate better with my fellow classmates. I have gotten better at doing presentations, learning how to make it engaging for the audience.

Final Sendoff

Thank you for exploring my digital portfolio. I hope that I have given you insight on who I am as a person, what I aspire to be, and what I work I created to make my goals clear. Having my senior and final year of high school coming to a close is really bittersweet, however I really look forward to using the skills I have learned through school and the skills I have taught myself in my future career and occupation. In El Camino College, I will continue my ambition to become an engineer. My dream in life is to become a part of a development team that is tasked to create a car that you and the next generation of drivers will drive on the open road and enjoy it.

I myself will continue to make mistakes and learn from them. When designing a new product, an engineer will create mistakes, whether it being a bolt being a millimeter off, or a separation mechanism for a stainless steel rocket that costed upwards of 10 billion dollars to develop. However, it is the engineer's responsibility to learn from those mistakes and apply what they have learned into their next big project. Like what Steve Jobs (Founder of Apple) said in his 2005 Stanford Commencement Address, Jobs says to, "Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish."
